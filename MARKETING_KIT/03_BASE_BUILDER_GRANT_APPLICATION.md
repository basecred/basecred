### Question 1: What is your project and what problem does it solve on Base?
BaseCred is a decentralized on-chain reputation and content curation protocol built natively for the Base L2 ecosystem. Traditional token distributions and airdrops are plagued by mercenary bot swarms and zero-effort social tipping spam. 

BaseCred solves this with a two-pillar architecture:
1. **8-Factor On-Chain Footprint Verification:** Evaluates wallets transparently across cumulative gas burnt, Ethereum L1 vintage, Base nonce longevity, organic multi-month consistency, Portal bridge verification, Basename ownership, and anti-Sybil screening to allocate cryptographic credits ($CRED).
2. **Weekly Base Spotlight (Curate-to-Earn):** Grants users exactly one non-stackable pass per week to endorse top educational, analytical, or developer content tagging `@base`. Passes can be used to curate or traded on a decentralized P2P marketplace.

### Question 2: How does BaseCred drive growth for the Base ecosystem?
1. **Accelerates Basename Adoption:** Holders of `.base.eth` domains receive significant reputation weight, directly driving Basename registrations and primary mint volume.
2. **Ecosystem Protocol Recognition:** Directly incentivizes historic and ongoing liquidity and contract interaction across Base protocols (Aerodrome, Uniswap on Base, Moonwell).
3. **High-Effort Content Incentive:** The 2x Viral Multiplier (when content is recognized by `@base` or `@jessepollak`) aligns creators and community members to publish in-depth tutorials, dashboard analytics, and developer guides.
4. **Public Good Architecture:** Zero proprietary lock-in. Full Merkle tree generators and bitmap smart contracts are MIT-licensed and reproducible by any Base developer.
